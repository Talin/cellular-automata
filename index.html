About Cellular Automata

Cellular automata are discrete mathematical models that demonstrate how complex patterns and behaviors can emerge from simple rules. Each cell in a grid follows basic rules based on its neighbors' states.

The Beauty of Emergence

Watch as simple rules create intricate, unpredictable patterns. From Conway's Game of Life to Wolfram's elementary rules, cellular automata reveal the hidden complexity in simplicity.

Explore and Create

Use the controls to customize rules, draw your own patterns, or load classic examples. Experiment with different configurations to discover unique behaviors and fascinating emergent structures.
